On 12 June 2009, Iran held a presidential election. Officials declared incumbent Mahmoud Ahmadinejad the winner, while challengers Mir Hossein Mousavi and Mehdi Karroubi alleged fraud. Mousavi urged supporters to protest, and large demonstrations followed in Tehran and other cities under the slogan "Where is my vote?" The protests, named the Green Movement after Mousavi's campaign colour, continued for months.

<!--more-->

Britannica describes the protests as the largest in Iran since the 1979 revolution. Amnesty International said in June 2010 that up to 80 people, and possibly many more, died during and after the demonstrations, including from torture in detention. Thousands were arrested, and a mass trial of more than 100 detainees was broadcast before Ahmadinejad's second inauguration.

In February 2011, Mousavi and Karroubi were placed under house arrest. The movement effectively ended that year, but Britannica notes that Iranians returned to the streets in later years.
