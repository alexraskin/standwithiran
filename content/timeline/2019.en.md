On 15 November 2019, protests erupted across Iran after the government announced an overnight rise in fuel prices. Demonstrations spread to more than 100 cities and turned to broader anger over economic hardship and governance. Security forces responded with live ammunition between 15 and 19 November.

<!--more-->

On 16 November, authorities imposed a near-total internet shutdown. NetBlocks reported connectivity flatlined at 5% for several days before rising to 64% on 23 November. Amnesty International verified the deaths of 323 people killed by security services between 15 and 19 November, and said the real number was likely higher. Reuters reported a much higher toll of about 1,500, provided by three Iranian interior ministry officials.

No official has been held accountable, according to Amnesty International. The shutdown and the differing tolls mean the full scale remains disputed.
