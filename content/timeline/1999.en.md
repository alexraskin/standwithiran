On 8 July 1999, a few hundred Tehran University students protested peacefully against the closure of the reformist newspaper *Salam* and proposed restrictions on the press. At about 4.30am on 9 July, Human Rights Watch reported, police allowed members of Ansar-e Hezbollah to enter a student dormitory. Armed police then entered, firing in the air and using tear gas, while dormitory rooms were ransacked and students assaulted.

<!--more-->

Human Rights Watch, citing the independent newspaper *Neshat*, reported 300 students wounded, 400 detained and four killed in the assault. Amnesty International, in a statement hosted by the Abdorrahman Boroumand Center, reported at least one student killed and said hundreds, possibly thousands, were arrested in Tehran and other centres as protests spread.

Amnesty International also reported that dozens of detained students were tortured at Towhid prison, at least 14 people received prison terms and four received death sentences, later overturned. The date, 18 Tir in the Iranian calendar, is still commemorated.
