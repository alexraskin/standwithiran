On 28 February 2026, the United States and Israel began strikes on Iran, called Operation Epic Fury by the United States and Operation Roaring Lion by Israel, according to the Congressional Research Service (CRS). Israel killed Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, CRS reported. Iran retaliated with missiles and drones against Israel and across the region, and moved to control the Strait of Hormuz.

<!--more-->

CRS, citing HRANA, reported 3,636 people killed in Iran as of 7 April 2026, including 1,701 civilians, and 18 US service members killed. No complete toll for the war exists in these sources. Human Rights Watch reported a US strike on a primary school in Minab on 28 February killed more than 160 people; the UN Fact-Finding Mission put that strike at over 150 killed.

The United States and Iran agreed an April ceasefire and signed a June memorandum, CRS reported. It collapsed weeks later, expired in August, and limited strikes had resumed by September.
