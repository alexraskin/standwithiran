Jina Mahsa Amini, a 22-year-old Iranian-Kurdish woman, died in morality police custody in Tehran in September 2022, after her arrest for alleged non-observance of mandatory hijab laws. The UN Fact-Finding Mission later found physical violence in custody led to her unlawful death. Protests under the slogan "Woman, Life, Freedom" began on 16 September 2022 and spread across Iran.

<!--more-->

The UN Fact-Finding Mission cited credible figures of as many as 551 protesters killed by security forces, including at least 49 women and 68 children, most by firearms. Iran Human Rights independently verified at least 551 killed, including 68 children and 49 women, in 26 provinces.

The Mission found many violations amounted to crimes against humanity, including murder and gender persecution. It said at least nine young men were executed after summary trials between December 2022 and January 2024, and repression of supporters continued.
